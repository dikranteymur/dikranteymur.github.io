Questions about an order, your Duble Wallet or Coffee Beans? Ask us at the counter, or email us. We usually reply within 1–2 business days.

Frequently asked questions

How do I pick a pickup time?
At checkout, under Pickup, choose As soon as possible or a time later today or tomorrow. The times follow the café's opening hours.
Something was wrong with my order.
Tell the staff at the counter, or email us with the pickup code. Refunds go back to the card or Duble Wallet you paid with.
How do I pay at the counter?
Open Pay and show the code. The code changes every 30 seconds, so a screenshot won't work. After staff scan it, confirm the payment and tip on your phone.
Someone may have a photo of my wallet code.
In Pay, tap ⋯ → Replace card number. Your balance moves to a new number and the old one stops working. If you added the card to Apple Wallet or Google Wallet, add it again.
When do my Coffee Beans expire?
Six months after you earn them, oldest first (birthday Beans after 30 days). Gold Bean members keep theirs. You'll get a reminder 30 and 7 days before. See the Coffee Beans rules.
I didn't get the sign-in code.
Check the number and wait a minute before asking for a new code. If you replied STOP to our messages earlier, reply START to receive codes again. You can also sign in with Apple or Google.
How do I stop offer notifications?
Turn off Profile → Offers & news notifications. Order and wallet notifications still come through.
How do I get a copy of my data or delete my account?
Use Profile → Download my data or Profile → Delete account. See the Privacy Policy for what's kept after deletion.
