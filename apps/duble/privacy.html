Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

  • Privacy Policy
  • Terms of Use
  • Support

Duble is the app of Duble Coffee & Beyond, a café at 965 Providence Square Shopping Center, Virginia Beach, Virginia ("Duble", "we", "us"). With it you can order ahead, pay with the Duble Wallet and collect Coffee Beans. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. It applies to the Duble apps for iPhone (com.mdteymur.duble) and Android.

In short: we collect what we need to run your account, take your orders and payments and run Coffee Beans. We don't sell your personal information, we don't show ads and the app contains no analytics or tracking tools.

Information we collect

  • Account: your phone number (we text you a sign-in code) or, if you sign in with Apple or Google, the account identifier and email address they share with us. Your first and last name, and your birthday if you add it (used for the birthday bonus).
  • Orders: what you order, your notes, your chosen pickup time, totals, tips, rewards used and order status.
  • Duble Wallet: your Duble Wallet card number, balance and activity (money added, payments, refunds).
  • Payments: card details are entered into Square's payment form, or come from Apple Pay or Google Pay. They go straight to Square, our payment processor. We never receive or store your full card number; for saved cards we see only the brand, last four digits and expiry date that Square returns.
  • Coffee Beans: beans earned, spent and expired, your tier, rewards redeemed, your invite code and who invited you.
  • Counter payments: when staff scan your code at the counter, the amount, tip and reward, and which staff member took the payment.
  • Notifications: a push notification token for your device, and on iPhone a Live Activity token while an order is in progress.
  • Security: IP addresses and the type of request (for example a failed sign-in) to stop abuse. These records are deleted after 90 days.

The app does not use your location, contacts, camera or photos. The Directions button opens a maps app of your choice; we don't see your location.

How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To take, prepare and hand over your orders, and to tell you when they're ready.
  • To add money to and pay from your Duble Wallet, and to show your balance and activity.
  • To run Coffee Beans: earning, rewards, tiers, the birthday bonus and invites, and to remind you before beans expire.
  • To send offers and news by push notification, if you leave that option on (see Your choices).
  • To answer support requests, prevent fraud and abuse, keep records required by law, and understand which products sell, in aggregate, to run the café.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that run parts of the app for us, and only what they need:

  • Square (Block, Inc.): payments, saved cards, the Duble Wallet gift card, orders and a customer record with your name and phone number, so your app orders show at the café's register.
  • Twilio: sends the sign-in code to your phone number.
  • Apple and Google: Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, push notifications, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes if you add one.
  • Railway: hosts our server and database in the United States.

Café staff see your first name, last initial, order and, when they scan your code, your wallet balance and Coffee Beans. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale of the business, in which case this policy continues to apply. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your profile, Coffee Beans history, notification tokens and saved sign-in sessions are deleted. Orders and payment records are kept without your name, because tax and accounting rules require them. Any Duble Wallet balance stays on its card number and can still be spent at the counter. Square keeps its own records under Square's privacy notice.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and download: in the app, go to Profile → Download my data to get a copy of the information we keep about you.
  • Correct: change your name in Profile → Edit profile. For anything else, email us.
  • Delete: go to Profile → Delete account, or email us.
  • Offers and news: turn off Profile → Offers & news notifications. Notifications about your orders, wallet and Coffee Beans still go out. You can turn off all notifications in your phone's settings.

Depending on where you live, including Virginia and California, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold, to get a copy of it, to correct or delete it, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising (we do neither). You can use these rights in the app or by emailing mdtsoftware21@gmail.com. We will confirm your request using your account and reply within 45 days. If we decline a request, you can appeal by replying to our answer with "Appeal" in the subject; if you're not satisfied with the outcome of the appeal, Virginia residents can contact the Virginia Attorney General. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Security

Information travels over encrypted connections. Sign-in tokens are stored in your phone's secure storage (Keychain on iPhone, encrypted storage on Android). No system is perfectly secure; if you think your account or your wallet code has been exposed, replace your card number in Pay → Replace card number and contact us.

Children

Duble is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
