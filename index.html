About

Small studio, one person

MDT Software Apps is the name Dikran Teymur publishes under. There is no team behind the apps: the same developer designs them, writes the code, tests them and answers the support email.

Each app does a specific job. Tune an instrument accurately. Keep track of what you spend. Put a café's menu on a TV without a cloud subscription. Or spend a few quiet minutes on a puzzle.

The apps are made for iPhone and Android, and some also run on iPad, Mac, Apple Watch or a TV. Where an app isn't in a store yet, its card says so.

  • No account needed to playTilted Shelf and Sliding Penguins save your progress on your device. There is no sign-up.
  • Audio stays on your deviceProfessional Tuner analyses the microphone signal on your phone. No audio is recorded or uploaded.
  • Menus stay on your networkScreen Menu sends menus from your device to your TV over local Wi‑Fi, without a cloud server.
  • Every data practice is written downEach app's privacy policy lists exactly which services it uses, including ads and purchases.

Contact

For questions, bug reports and feedback about any app:

mdtsoftware21@gmail.com

Emails are usually answered within 1–2 business days.

Reporting a problem

Please include:

  • App name
  • App version
  • Device model
  • iOS or Android version
  • What you did, step by step
  • What happened, with a screenshot if possible

You can find the app version in the app's settings screen, or in the App Store or Google Play listing on your device.
