By creating an account or using the app, you agree to these terms and to our Privacy Policy.

1. Your account

You must be at least 13 years old to create an account. Keep your phone and sign-in secure; you're responsible for orders and payments made from your account. One account per person. We may suspend accounts used for fraud, abuse or to break these terms.

2. Text messages

When you sign in with your phone number, we text you a one-time sign-in code. You'll receive one message each time you request a code. Message and data rates may apply. We don't send marketing by text message. Reply STOP to stop messages or HELP for help; without text messages you can still sign in with Apple or Google.

3. Ordering ahead

  • Prices, taxes and the total are shown before you pay. Menu items and prices can change, and items can sell out.
  • You can ask for your order as soon as possible or choose a pickup time from the times offered. We prepare it for that time, but times are estimates.
  • Orders are paid when you place them. Pick up at the counter with your pickup code. Orders not collected by closing time may be discarded without a refund.
  • If we can't fill your order, we'll refund what you paid to the card or Duble Wallet you used. For a problem with an order, talk to staff at the café or email us.
  • Tips go to the café's team.
  • Allergen information is available from staff. Our kitchen handles common allergens, so we can't guarantee any item is free of them.

4. Duble Wallet

  • The Duble Wallet is a stored-value card (a Square gift card) for use at Duble. You add money with a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay, from $5 to $250 at a time, up to a $500 balance.
  • Your balance does not expire, and there are no fees for having or using it.
  • The balance can be spent on app orders and at the counter. It is not a bank account or credit, earns no interest, and can't be transferred to another account or exchanged for cash, except where the law requires it.
  • Treat your wallet code like cash: anyone who has it may be able to start a payment, which you then confirm in the app. If you think someone has a photo of it, use Pay → Replace card number; your balance moves to the new number.
  • If you delete your account, your balance stays on its card number and can still be spent at the counter.

5. Coffee Beans

  • Earning: you earn 1 Bean for each $1 added to your Duble Wallet, plus a bonus for bigger reloads (5 Beans from $25, 15 from $50, 40 from $100) and 25 Beans for your first reload. Duble tier members earn 10% more and Gold Bean members 25% more on reloads. Beans aren't earned on orders themselves, tips or taxes.
  • Tiers: based on Beans earned in the last 12 months: Single from 0, Duble from 300, Gold Bean from 1,000. Gold Bean members also get one free extra each calendar month.
  • Rewards: Free Extra (30 Beans), Single Drink (100), Duble Drink (150) and Dessert (200), in the app or at the counter.
  • Birthday: add your birthday to get a Single Drink's worth of Beans on your birthday. Birthday Beans last 30 days. You can change your birthday once.
  • Invites: when a friend joins with your code and adds money for the first time, you each get 50 Beans, for up to 10 friends every 12 months.
  • Expiry: Beans expire 6 months after you earn them, oldest first; at most 100 expire on any one day, and Gold Bean members keep theirs. We'll notify you 30 and 7 days before Beans expire.
  • Coffee Beans have no cash value, can't be sold or transferred and aren't property. Beans earned by mistake, fraud or abuse may be removed. A phone number is needed to collect and use Beans. Beans end when you delete your account.
  • We may change or end Coffee Beans; we'll give at least 30 days' notice in the app before a change that reduces the value of Beans you already have.
